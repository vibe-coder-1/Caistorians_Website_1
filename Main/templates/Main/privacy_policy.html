Privacy Policy

The Caistorians' Association is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and how it is stored.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information including your name, email address, and any details you choose to include when submitting forms on this website. We do not collect unnecessary personal data.

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through this website is used solely to respond to enquiries, manage alumni communications, and support association activities. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Data Storage and Security

Data is stored securely and access is limited to authorised student administrators. Reasonable technical and organisational measures are in place to protect against unauthorised access or misuse.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Requests can be made via the contact page.

Cookies

This website uses only essential cookies required for basic functionality. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

This policy may be updated periodically. Last updated: December 2025.
