The Caistorians' association was created in December of 2025 by a small group of students. Our goal is to create a platform which connects aluni from all years, allowing them to share stories, photos, and stay in touch with upcoming events. We believe that by fostering a strong alumni community, we can support current students, provide mentorship opportunities, and create a network that benefits every generation of Caistorians.
